For questions about a record, an order, shipping or returns, eBay messaging is the fastest and safest way to reach Music Ed Cafe.

Message through eBay

  1. Open the record's eBay listing: every record page here has a link to it. Or go to the Music Ed Cafe store on eBay.
  2. Choose Contact seller on a listing, or Contact on the store page.
  3. Ask anything you like: extra photos, a play-graded sound check, a digitized audio sample, or pressing details such as catalog or runout numbers.

Using eBay keeps your conversation attached to the listing, and any questions about an order are covered by eBay's buyer protection.
