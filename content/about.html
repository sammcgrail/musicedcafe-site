Music Ed Cafe is an independent vinyl record seller based in Lynn, Massachusetts. Every record is sold online through the Music Ed Cafe store on eBay.

Every record has a story

In the words of the Music Ed Cafe eBay store: every record has a story and every purchase helps support music education and the preservation of analog sound. The goal is simple: connect great music with great listeners, and help preserve the history of recorded sound. Whether you're a seasoned collector, new to vinyl, a DJ or hunting for one particular album, you're welcome here.

How records are prepared

  • Records are cleaned before listing, with an ultrasonic cleaner and a Spin Doctor.
  • Each listing has clear photos of the exact copy you'll receive.
  • Condition is described honestly, with the vinyl and the sleeve graded separately.
  • Digitized audio samples are available on request before you buy.
  • Orders are packed securely and shipped quickly.

Music Ed Cafe is also building a YouTube channel, where select records are digitized and shared with audio samples and album artwork.

Good Coffee, Good Music, Good People

The name says a lot. Coffee and records go together, and so do the slogans on the Music Ed Cafe poster: Good Coffee, Good Music, Good People, Support Local, Support Each Other, and Spin it. Feel it. Live it.

A real cafe, with coffee and crates under one roof, is a someday dream. For now, Music Ed Cafe is online only. There's no storefront or opening hours yet, and every purchase happens through eBay.
