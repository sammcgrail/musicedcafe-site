Quick listing (Edwin's rule) and pressing checks

    
Quick-pick lists every "review" record on its most likely release (the record's country and year, then the closest format, then the earliest year at or after the release year, then the most Discogs "haves"). Each comment says "Pressing variant not verified; see photos on the listing page." until the runout is checked on Verify pressing. Nothing is listed until "List on Discogs" is on.
