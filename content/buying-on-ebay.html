This website is a catalog. Every purchase is completed on eBay, which handles payment, shipping and returns.

How it works

  1. Find a record here. Browse by genre or format, or search the shop.
  2. Tap Buy on eBay. It opens that record's exact eBay listing.
  3. Check out on eBay. Pay with the methods eBay offers at checkout. Shipping cost, delivery estimate and the return policy are all shown on the listing.

What eBay handles

  • Payment: processed by eBay. This website never asks for card details, and never takes payment.
  • Shipping: postage is calculated on the listing, and tracking appears in your eBay purchase history.
  • Returns: each listing shows its return policy, and returns are opened through eBay.
  • Buyer protection: eligible purchases are covered by eBay's Money Back Guarantee.

Prices and availability

The eBay listing is always the final word. Prices, offers and stock are set on eBay and can change between site updates. If a record has just sold, its page here shows it as sold and suggests similar records that are still available.

About the pictures

Most records show Music Ed Cafe's own photos of the exact copy for sale. Where those aren't available on this site yet, a record may show stock cover art, marked "Stock cover". That artwork comes from the Cover Art Archive and MusicBrainz (or Apple iTunes when they have none). It shows what the release looks like in general. It's not a photo of this copy, and it doesn't tell you which pressing it is. Always check the eBay listing for the actual photos and condition.
